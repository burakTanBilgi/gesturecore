Readouts

How often the numbers are allowed to change. Bars always move at full frame rate — this is only the digits, which cannot be read if they change faster than about once a second.

Every change goes straight to core.setConfig() and is saved in this browser only. ◆ marks values that differ from the defaults. Where a slider is a threshold on something measurable, the grey needles on its track are that measurement right now, one per finger per hand — drag the handle through them to see what the threshold will and will not catch. The rest are times and filter constants, which have nothing live to stand against.

Gestures are plain data: poses are hand shapes, movements are how the hand travels. Both take effect on the next frame and are saved with the config.

Record a pose from your hand

Hold the hand shape. The recorder averages each finger's curl and makes a pose that matches your curls ± the tolerance. A new pose appears in Moves straight away, and fires a pose event once it beats every other pose.

Tick include thumb for any shape the thumb defines — a thumbs-up is a fist to every other finger, so without the thumb it is scored as fist and never wins.

Record a movement

Show it instead of describing it. Perform the movement a few times; the bench watches which way your hand actually travelled, how far and how long it took, and fits a movement that every take would have fired — then checks that against the takes before offering it.

Cues (JSON)

One-shot sounds for gesture events. on is an event type; hand, name and finger narrow it; pan can be "hand".

Controls (JSON)

Hand measurements steering a voice while a condition holds. Controls with the same voice steer the same voice.

gesturecore-head, an optional brick: it reads your face from the same camera — where your head points, nods and shakes, blinks and winks, raised brows, an open mouth, a smile. The hands work the same with it off. Switching it on downloads the face model (3.8 MB) and runs it on every frame.

Neutral

Angles start from facing the camera squarely. If your screen sits to one side, or you blink-read as half closed, sit as you normally do, look at the screen and press Set neutral: that pose becomes zero and those eyes count as open.

Settings, expressions and movements (JSON)

An expression is ranges on readings, all holding at once: degrees for yaw, pitch, roll; 0…1 for the rest, including any raw blendshape by name. A movement is strokes of one angle, like a hand movement but in degrees.

gesturecore-chords, an optional brick: two hands play a chord. The note hand counts the scale on its fingers — one finger is A, and each finger after it steps up — while the other hand decides major or minor by pinching its middle finger. Tilt the note hand past 20° to sharpen or flatten. Nothing is downloaded and nothing needs switching on; it reads the poses and pinches the core already reports.

Playing

Three of the letters name poses the core does not ship — peace, three and four. They are greyed out above until you record them in Moves, and until then the scale has holes in it. Whichever hand is not the note hand carries quality, and latch is the trigger to try first — it ignores the tracking drops the others have to ride out.

Letters, quality and accidentals (JSON)

Every letter maps to a shape: { "pose": "fist" } or { "pinch": "index" }. The same goes for minor, on whichever hand is not the note hand. accidental takes enterDeg to pick a sharp up and exitDeg to put it down — two angles, so a hand resting near the threshold cannot flap between them.

False activations per 10 minutes

This measures the core rather than adding a feature: while you use the computer normally, how often does gesturecore fire a gesture you did not mean? The target is fewer than 2 per 10 minutes. If it fails, the defaults need retuning (usually pinch.closed, hysteresis, dwellMs, engage.dwellMs).

  1. Start the camera and sit as you normally do, hands in view.
  2. Press Start below.
  3. Work normally for at least 10 minutes: type, reach for things, rest your hands, and gesture on purpose now and then.
  4. Every time the log shows a pinch or movement you did not intend, press F.

Test captures: a recorded hand the automated tests replay, so a change in the maths shows up as a failing test instead of a surprise. Saving writes test/fixtures/<name>.json; run npm test afterwards.

This does not add a gesture. To teach the bench a new hand shape so it fires events, use Gestures → Record a pose from your hand. Capture it here as well only if you want the tests to guard it.

What to capture
Views

One snapshot proves nothing: hand tracking is weakest away from a square-on view, so the same shape is recorded from several angles and distances and the tests require all of them to read the same. Capture what you can — every view you add tightens the test.

mirror (header) flips the image like a mirror, so moving your hand right moves it right on screen. It also flips the x coordinates the core receives, which affects tilt sign and swipe direction.

swap L/R (header) only changes the Left/Right label. Raise only your right hand: it should say Right. The two settings are independent.
